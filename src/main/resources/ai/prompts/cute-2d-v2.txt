Render the existing artwork as a cute, clean, flat 2D illustration.

Express cuteness through gentle line quality, tidy contours and smooth source-colored regions, not through new anatomy or redesigned proportions. Preserve the original outer silhouette, relative sizes and internal structure.

If a face is clearly present, gently soften the rendering of its existing features while preserving their number, placement, size, expression and one-sided details. If a head or body is clearly present, refine its linework without enlarging or reshaping it. If these features are absent or ambiguous, leave them absent or ambiguous.

Keep abstract marks and open line drawings abstract and open. Do not convert them into a cute creature or complete them into a character.

Use the source palette. Do not replace saturated colors with pastels or color unfilled regions. Use little to no shading and retain a clearly flat illustrated appearance.

No sculpted volume, realistic materials, glossy reflections or three-dimensional rendering. Follow the base preservation priorities whenever a cuter treatment would change the original design.
